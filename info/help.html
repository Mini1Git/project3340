Do you need help?

General

Do you have physical locations?

No, we are an online-only delivery service.

How can I contact customer support?

You can contact our customer support team by emailing us at support@grillow.com or by calling our hotline at 1-123-4567.

What are your business hours?

Our customer support team is available 24/7 to assist you. Each Restaurant has their own personal hours.

Account

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login page and click on the "Forgot Password" link. Follow the instructions to receive a password reset email.

How do I update my email address?

To update your email address, go to your account settings and look for the "Email" section. Enter your new email address and save the changes.

How can I become an administrator account?

To become an administrator account, you need to be either an authorized restaurant manager, or driver. You can apply your restaurant to work with us at "Partner with Us" page, and become a driver in the "Become a driver" Page

Orders

How do I track my order?

You can track your order by logging into your account and navigating to the "My Orders" section.

How do I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order by logging into your account and navigating to the "My Orders" section. Select the order you wish to cancel and follow the instructions.

How do I get a refund?

If your order was damaged, stolen, or doesn't meet our expectations, youcan request a refund by logging into your account and navigating to the "My Orders" section. Select the order you wish to refund and follow the instructions.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple pay, Paypal, and Google pay.

How do I update my payment method?

To update your payment method, go to your account settings and look for the "Payment" section. Enter your new payment information and save the changes.

Can I get a gift card?

You can purchase gift cards from our participating partner restaurants. Gift cards can be used to make purchases online or in-store.

Services

What Services do you offer?

We offer a wide range of services to meet your needs, including delivery, pickup, and dine-in options.

When are your services available?

Our services are available 24/7, but specific availability may vary by Restaurant location, policy, and service type.

Delivery

How does delivery work?

Our delivery service allows you to have your favourite meals delivered right to your doorstep. You can track your order in real-time and receive notifications about its status. We have a team of dedicated drivers with thorough background checks and training, who ensure that your food arrives fresh and on time.

What is the delivery radius?

Delivery is available from anywhere within Windsor. You can check if we deliver to your location during the checkout process.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time can vary depending on your location and the restaurant's current order volume. You can view estimated delivery times during the checkout process.

Still need help?

If you have any other questions or need assistance, feel free to contact our customer support team. We're here to help! Our email is support@grillow.com, and our phone number is 1-123-4567.
