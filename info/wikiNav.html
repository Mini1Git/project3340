Site Navigation Guide

Using the Side bar

The primary way to move through Grillow is via the Side Navigation Bar. On desktop, this is visible on the left. On mobile devices, click the Hamburger Icon (three lines) in the top left corner to toggle the menu open and closed.

Home

Main page showing the site description, and the featured restaurants.

Browse

Page where you can see all restaurants, and search for specific ones.

Orders

Page where you can see your previous orders, and view the order details about an order.

Favourites

Page where you can see your most frequently ordered Restaurants

Cart

Page where you can see what products are in your cart, and you can go to checkout. To add an item to your cart simply go to a restaurant menu, and press the plus button.

Help

Page that helps customers by answering frequently asked questions, and provides help contact information in case they need more help.

Partner with Us

Page where you can fill out a form to apply to be one of our restaurant partners. More details in Forms Wiki Page

Become a Driver

Page where you can fill out a form to apply to become a driver. More details in Forms.

About us

Some information about the Grillow company's goals, intentions, and team.

Sign up/ Log in

Buttons at the top left our website that allow you to log into an existing account, sign up to create an account, or log out of your account. More information about signing up in Forms Wiki.
