Site Theme Guide

The Three Site Templates

Grillow comes with three distinct visual themes that change the look and feel of the entire interface. These are controlled via external CSS files and a JavaScript theme switcher.

Classic Theme

Our signature look featuring vibrant red, orange, and white. This is designed for high-energy food discovery and matches our "rushing pizza" logo.

Dark Theme

A low-light template that uses black and classic Grillow Red accents. This is ideal for late-night browsers and reduces eye strain.

Blue Theme

A special blue template that uses colourful blue and purple. This theme is for those who want a bit more colour and fun in their life.

How theme switching works

Change themes with the button on the bottom right of the screen. Your theme choice is saved to your browser's session storage, so it will persist even if you refresh the page or navigate to another one. We use CSS variables to make it easy to switch between themes, or modify the themes.
