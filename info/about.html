About Us

The number 1 choice for Windsor food delivery

Grillow is a local Windsor food delivery service that connects customers with local restaurants and delivery drivers. We are committed to providing the best possible experience for our customers, restaurant partners, and delivery drivers.

We care about and value our drivers

Unlike most other delivery services, Grillow takes care of our drivers, ensuring they make a fair hourly wage on top of delivery fees and tips, while also covering their gas expenses, and providing insurance coverage.

Our Restaurants

We carefully choose high quality restaurants in Windsor, that meet our standards. We work closely with our restaurant partners to ensure they are satisfied with our service, and we are committed to providing the best possible experience for all our partners.
