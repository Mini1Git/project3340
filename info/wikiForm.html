Site Form Guide

Sign Up

When a new user fills out this form (Name, Phone, Email, Password), the data is sent via a PHP POST request to our server.

The Database Action: The system checks if the email already exists in the users table. If it's unique, it creates a new record.

Security: Passwords are never stored as plain text; they are "hashed" (scrambled) before being saved in the database to ensure user privacy.

Log In

Authentication: This form compares the entered credentials against the stored records in the database.

User Sessions: Once verified, the PHP backend starts a "Session." This tells the website to "remember" who you are as you move from the Home page to the Cart, so you don't have to log in again every time you click a button.

Partner With Us

This form is for local Windsor business owners who want to list their menu on Grillow.

Data Collection: We collect the Restaurant Name, Cuisine Type, Restaurant Email, Restaurant phone number, and Restaurant address.

Dynamic Setup: Once approved, the restaurant is assigned a unique restaurant_id, which allows the owner to start adding products to their specific digital storefront.

Become a Driver

We currently are not hiring more drivers, but as we plan to expand in the future we will be needing more drivers!

For now, the form takes the Driver email, and Driver resume, and if the Grillow staff are interested in you they will contact you for future details.

How forms work

The Input: A user types information into a box (the Form).

The Delivery: When they click "Submit," a PHP script "picks up" that data and carries it to the server.

The Storage: The server opens the MySQL Database and places the information in the correct table.

The Result: The next time the user visits their profile, the website "reads" that file and displays their information back to them.

Error Handling

Ensure all "Required" fields are filled out. Our forms also have conditions such as emails must not already be used, passwords must have capital and lowercase, number, symbol, and at least 10 characters long. Phone number and email must be valid. Our JavaScript checks this before the data even leaves the user's browser.
