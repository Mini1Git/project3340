Get to know the Creators

Grillow was started by University of Windsor students Wilson Tran, Kayden Ions, Nazifa Tahsin, and Liana Bell. We weren't satisfied with the current food delivery options in Windsor, and wanted to create a better experience for customers, restaurants, and drivers. We are passionate about food and technology, and we are committed to providing the best possible experience for our customers, restaurant partners, and delivery drivers.
